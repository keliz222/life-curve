解How it works

  1. Year乙酉 Month戊子 Day辛巳 Hour壬辰

    1Birth → 8 characters

    Your birth year, month, day, and hour each become two Chinese characters: your Bazi 八字.

  2. 2Five elements

    Each character is Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, or Water. Like rock-paper-scissors, some help you and some strain you.

  3. 3Every year scores

    Each year brings its own elements. Helpful years rise, hard ones dip. Your own events bend it further.

What is Bazi? Learn more
天干 地支
Stems and branches. Every year, month, day, and hour has a top character (10 stems) and a bottom one (12 branches, the zodiac animals).
日主
Day master. The top character of your day pillar. It stands for you.
强 弱
Strong or weak. A strong chart is helped by what drains it; a weak one by what supports it.
大运 流年
Luck cycles and years. A new cycle every 10 years sets the decade's tone; each year adds its own.

Bazi is a cultural tradition, not science. This site is for fun and reflection.

    How does this chart become my curve?

    Every year starts at a score of 50. Then:

    • Your 10-year luck cycle (大运) adds or takes away up to 20 points, depending on whether its elements help you.
    • That year's own pillar (流年) adds or takes away up to 14 points the same way.
    • Special meetings between that year's branch and your chart move it a few points: a clash 冲 (−6), a combination 合 (+4), a punishment 刑 (−5), or a three-way merge 三合 (up to ±7).
    • Your own events then push that year and later years, most of all in years of the same theme (career, money, study…).

    Tap any year on the curve and look at Why this score to see these parts for that year. The rules follow traditional Bazi ideas, but the exact point values are this site's own choices.
